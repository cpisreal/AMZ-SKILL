## 100-point self-check

### Popular-item structure match, 40 points

- Category and competitor alignment, 10
- Product quality and attribute clarity, 10
- Offer and price fit, 10
- Keyword and browse discoverability, 10

### Click attraction, 20 points

- Main image clarity, 8
- Search-result differentiation, 5
- Title click value, 4
- Price or coupon trigger, 3

### Conversion readiness, 20 points

- Trust proof, 5
- Detail-page completeness, 5
- Benefit clarity, 5
- Friction removal, 5

### Cold-start momentum, 20 points

- Early traffic quality, 5
- Early conversion signal, 5
- Engagement trend, 5
- Campaign and optimization speed, 5

## Interpretation

- 80-100: strong readiness
- 65-79: possible with fixes
- 50-64: weak or unstable
- 0-49: unlikely

## Fast diagnosis logic

- Low structure score: align product, price, offer, category, and keywords with similar popular items.
- Low click score: fix main image, title, coupon, and visible differentiation.
- Low conversion score: fix trust proof, A+ content, objection handling, and offer clarity.
- Low momentum score: improve launch ads, keyword quality, traffic relevance, and response speed.

## Report reminder

Never guarantee the badge. Describe readiness and probability qualitatively.
