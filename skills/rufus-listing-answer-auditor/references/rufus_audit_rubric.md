# Rufus Audit Rubric and Templates

## Core check

A listing is Rufus-ready when it supports this path:

```text
question -> answer -> noun phrase -> semantic association -> product selection -> click validation
```

## Noun phrase patterns

Use natural phrases that combine several useful signals:

- [attribute] + [category]: dual zone car refrigerator
- [power/compatibility] + [category]: 12v portable fridge freezer
- [use case] + [category]: camping fridge for rv travel
- [problem] + [solution]: no ice electric cooler
- [feature] + [outcome]: wheels and handle for easy transport
- [contents] + [storage mode]: frozen food and drink storage

## Question types

- What is good for [use case]?
- How do I solve [problem]?
- Can this product [performance task]?
- Does it work with [vehicle/device/environment]?
- Is it better than [substitute]?
- Is it easy to [install/use/move/clean]?
- What accessories or replacements do I need?

## Rewrite pattern

```text
Question: [natural shopper question]
Direct answer: [truthful answer]
Noun phrases: [3-6 useful n-grams]
Listing module: [title/bullet/q&a/image/a+]
Suggested copy: [paste-ready copy]
Proof needed: [yes/no + what proof]
```

## Score interpretation

- 0-1: missing, vague, or misleading.
- 2: present but not answer-ready.
- 3: clear enough for baseline relevance.
- 4: strong answer-ready content with natural noun phrases.
- 5: category-leading clarity with proof, use cases, and tight semantic bridges.

## Backend keyword allocation guide

Use as a starting point, not a rule:

- 40% use-case and problem language
- 25% specification and compatibility terms
- 15% substitutes and comparison terms
- 10% complement and accessory terms
- 10% synonyms and regional phrasing

Avoid repeating title phrases excessively and avoid misleading traffic.
