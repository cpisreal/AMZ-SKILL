# Amazon Listing title policy reference

## Effective policy summarized by the user

Effective July 27, 2026:

- Product Title: maximum 75 characters, including spaces.
- Item Highlights: maximum 125 characters, including spaces.
- The two fields preserve a combined 200-character product-description space.
- Keep key product-identifying information in the title; move additional details to Item Highlights.
- Item Highlights may contain materials, important product features, comparison-relevant facts, and recommended use cases.
- Item Highlights is searchable and may appear with the title in search results and on product detail pages.
- Do not infer that Item Highlights and Product Title have equal search weight; Amazon has not specified relative weighting.

## Scope

The 2026 field limits apply to:

- all product types except media product types;
- Amazon stores except Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Türkiye, and the UAE.

Check any supplied marketplace and category requirements because a stricter or different rule may apply.

## Existing title rules still in force

The January 21, 2025 title requirements remain relevant:

- Do not use `!`, `$`, `?`, `_`, `{`, `}`, `^`, `¬`, or `¦` unless the character is part of the formal brand name.
- Do not repeat the same word more than twice, excluding articles, conjunctions, and prepositions.
- Keep titles clear, concise, and consistent.

The official wording concerns the same word, not necessarily a shared root. Use stemming only as a warning for possible keyword stuffing.

## Field responsibilities

| Field | Primary job |
|---|---|
| Product Title | Identify what the product is quickly and accurately |
| Item Highlights | Add material, major feature, compatibility, or recommended use case |
| Bullet Points | Explain benefits, specifications, usage, and purchase considerations |
| Product Description | Expand value, scenarios, and brand expression |
| Backend Search Terms | Hold relevant index terms unsuitable for natural front-end copy |

Item Highlights does not replace Bullet Points, Product Description, A+ Content, or backend search terms.

## Migration context

- Titles over 75 characters may be gradually replaced by Amazon AI recommendations.
- Listings remain active during this title-update process.
- Sellers may edit the title and Item Highlights.
- Brand owners receive a 14-day window to review, edit, and approve proposed AI-generated changes before implementation.

These migration details explain urgency but do not change the copywriting checks.

## Official example supplied in the source

Before:

`Premium Stainless Steel Insulated Water Bottle – Keeps Drinks Cold 24 Hours, Hot 12 Hours, BPA-Free`

Rewritten title:

`Premium Stainless Steel Insulated Water Bottle – BPA-Free`

Item Highlights:

`Keeps drinks cold 24 hours, hot 12 hours. Ideal for gym, office, and outdoor adventures.`

The example demonstrates reorganization, not truncation: product type, material, and an essential attribute remain in the title; performance and use cases move to Item Highlights.
